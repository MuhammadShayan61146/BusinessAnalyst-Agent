

## Typical capabilities of business-intelligence and analytics platforms

- **Data connectivity**: connectors to relational databases, cloud warehouses
  and file storage; scheduled refresh and incremental loading; clear handling
  of connection failures and credential rotation.
- **Data pipelines**: ingestion, cleaning and transformation steps with run
  history, retry rules and alerts when a pipeline fails.
- **Dashboards and visualisation**: drag-and-drop layout, a library of chart
  types, filters and drill-down, sharing and commenting, export to PDF or image.
- **KPIs and alerts**: metric definitions agreed with the business, refresh
  frequency, threshold and anomaly alerts, and a record of who was notified.
- **Reporting and distribution**: scheduled reports by email with access
  controls, so recipients only see data they are permitted to see.
- **Natural-language querying**: translating a question into a query needs
  guardrails: show the generated query, restrict to permitted data sources, and
  state when the answer may be incomplete.
- **Governance**: role-based access to data sources and dashboards, audit logs,
  and a documented owner for each dataset.
- **Common risks**: slow queries on large datasets, inconsistent metric
  definitions across teams, stale data, and exposure of sensitive data through
  shared dashboards.
